07 / DATA & METHODS

ALL DATA STAYS LOCAL

Every run uses a seeded random-number generator, shifted-force Lennard–Jones interactions, and velocity-Verlet integration. Export measurements or complete trajectories to analyze elsewhere.

Model scope. This two-dimensional, classical Lennard–Jones model is a pedagogical simplification: it omits molecular geometry, electrostatics, and directional hydrogen bonding. It does not reproduce the full water phase diagram or experimental water properties. Pressure and diffusion estimates are finite-size, finite-time approximations; equilibration uses simple velocity rescaling rather than a rigorous thermostat.
